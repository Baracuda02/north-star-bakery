Our Story

North Star Bakery started with a simple idea: bring people together through fresh, homemade baked goods. What began as a love for baking grew into a bakery focused on creating delicious breads, pastries, cakes, and other treats for our community. We believe that good food can make any day a little better, whether you are stopping in for a morning pastry, picking up a cake for a celebration, or enjoying a treat with friends and family.

Where our Ingredients come from?

At North Star Bakery, we believe that great baked goods start with great ingredients. We do our best to use fresh, high-quality ingredients and work with trusted suppliers whenever possible. We carefully choose our flour, fruits, dairy, and other ingredients to make sure our customers get products that taste fresh and homemade. We also believe in supporting local businesses and using locally sourced ingredients when they are available.

Meet Our Team

Our staff is what makes North Star Bakery special. From the bakers who prepare our products early each morning to the team members who welcome customers at the counter, everyone plays an important role. Our bakers bring creativity and attention to detail to every recipe, while our front-of-house team works hard to make every customer feel welcome. We are proud of our team and the care they put into everything we make and serve.
